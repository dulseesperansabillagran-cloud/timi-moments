Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Short version: TiMi Moments runs entirely in your browser. Your screenshots and posters never leave your device. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

1. What we process

TiMi Moments is a static web application. When you use it:

  • Screenshots you upload are read and rendered locally in your browser using standard web APIs. They are never transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • Text you type (player name, caption, activity title, activity link) is used only to draw your poster on your own device.
  • Generated posters exist in your browser's memory until you download or share them. Nothing is saved on a server.

2. What we do NOT do

  • We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not have user accounts, sign-ups, or databases.
  • We do not request or receive your Discord account information. The app does not call Discord APIs on your behalf and does not read your profile, servers, or messages.
  • We do not sell or share data, because we do not collect any.

3. When used inside Discord

If you launch TiMi Moments as an Activity inside Discord, Discord (not this app) handles your Discord session. Your interaction with Discord is governed by Discord's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. This app remains a static page loaded in a frame; it does not receive or store your Discord data.

4. Third-party links

Posters may contain a QR code pointing to an activity link you enter. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of the destination you choose.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the support server listed on our Discord application page, or open an issue on our public repository.
